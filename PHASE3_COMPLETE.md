## 📞 When NOT to Intervene

**System is self-managing when:**
- ✅ Single alert then recovers (normal)
- ✅ Memory grows then auto-cleans (normal)
- ✅ Occasional spike in response time (normal)
- ✅ TTS failure < 1% (acceptable)
- ✅ Any auto-recovery executes (desired)

**You only intervene if:**
- ❌ Same alert 3+ days in a row
- ❌ Auto-recovery not working (alerts remain)
- ❌ Memory continuously growing
- ❌ Service health "error" > 6 hours
